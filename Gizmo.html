Usage

Your 5 hours start when you send a message.

You get 100% every 5 hours, starting from your first message. Every answer uses some of it — bigger models use more, smaller ones less, and long answers or High effort use a little extra. When you reach 100% you'll need to wait for it to reset. There's also a generous weekly allowance that resets every Monday — you'll rarely reach it. Answers that run on your own device use a quarter as much.
