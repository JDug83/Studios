WHAT IS GAZSTUDIOS?

A collection of apps for curious people. Chat with AI, build a website with code, or take a break with a game. Choose what you want to do and open the app.

GazStudios brings our chat and coding tools together in one place. Use the browser apps straight from the website, with no account screen in the way.

Try a question in GazChat, explore a different conversation with Gizmo, or start building in GazCode. Choose the tool that fits what you want to do.

A few things to know.

Do I need an account?

No. The current GazStudios browser apps open without signing in. Any supported local saves stay in the browser and device you used.

Does every app run on my device?

Our browser tools open in your browser, but AI processing depends on your chosen model and setup. Some GazChat and Gizmo models need a model download or the GazChat server to be online.

Is GazCode available for Windows?

Yes. The Windows button links to the GazCode 26 executable on our GitHub release. The browser version is available separately.
